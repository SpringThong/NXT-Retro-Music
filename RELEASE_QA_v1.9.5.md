## Implementation and practical limits

The target is 60-Hz animation during actual movement, with short menu transitions over two bounded RAM snapshots, cached full-resolution artwork/icons/text, and bounded idle work. FFT sampling remains at its separate mode budget. No decoder/audio callback processing was added. CPU/thermal safeguards and user performance modes are retained; the build does not force maximum CPU to claim 60 FPS.

Frame rate, temperature, battery use, physical ROM boot, live Bluetooth/DAC/Native DSD/DoP and account services need device testing. A low-resolution source cover cannot gain real HD detail; new online fallback cover requests use 900×900, while embedded/existing art keeps its source quality. Existing 300px cached fallback covers are preserved, not silently re-downloaded.

Karaoke needs real Enhanced LRC timestamps from the supplied lyrics. The app does not derive syllable timing from audio or perform vocal scoring. Line-only lyrics remain line-synchronized, translations remain italic and paired. Sample UI fixture timings are explicitly authored, not advertised as song-accurate data.

The three official OTA IDs remain embedded. The publisher must upload the exact ROM installer and replace the contents of the existing Drive update.json while retaining its ID. Source ZIPs, OTA-kit ZIPs and unfilled placeholder manifests are not OTA app payloads.
