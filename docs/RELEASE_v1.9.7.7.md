# NXT Retro Music v1.9.7.7

**A personal music player built through vibe coding by Nguyễn Xuân Thông — Spring Thông.**

Choose the installer matching your ROM:

| ROM | Installer | App folder on SD card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock | `NXT_Retro_Music_v1.9.7.7_Stock.zip` | `Apps/NXT_Retro_Music/` |
| NextUI | `NXT_Retro_Music_v1.9.7.7_NextUI.zip` | `Tools/tg5040/NXT Retro Music.pak/` |
| NX Redux | `NXT_Retro_Music_v1.9.7.7_NXRedux.zip` | `Tools/NXT Retro Music.pak/` |

Close the app completely, back up your data, then extract the matching ZIP to the **SD card root**, merging `Apps` or `Tools`. The installers are the original v1.9.7.7 builds and are not rebuilt for this publication. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the original installers and source archive checksums.

This release refines icons, artwork scaling and alignment, improves Auralis active-state visibility, and retains the existing playback, DAC/DSD, EQ, lyrics/translation, profiles, library, LED and OTA features. Graphics caches are bounded and reused to reduce allocation work; no measured hardware FPS, temperature or battery guarantee is claimed.

Highlights: local music; Spotify Premium, SoundCloud, Google Drive, radio and podcasts; compatible USB DAC/DSD output; graphic and parametric EQ; bilingual lyrics with offline translation cache; Groq/MyMemory/Google providers; **51 themes, 60 fonts, 14 languages**; listener profiles; LED/spectrum customization; Wi-Fi file transfer; sleep-and-exit timer; ROM-specific OTA.

Brick Pro is the author's tested hardware. Brick and Brick Hammer require device/firmware confirmation. Services and DAC transports have their own requirements. See the full English/Vietnamese README for setup and limitations.
